# Knowledge base — entries that answer almost the same question

Some entries in `src/data/knowledge/` ask nearly the same question. When a visitor's wording sits between two of them, the assistant either answers with the neighbour or asks "Did you mean…?". This page lists every such pair we found and what we suggest for each.

**What we need from you:** sections 1 and 2. Reply with the numbers you approve (for example "merge 1, 2; all links"). Merging or changing an answer is only done with your OK. Section 3 needs nothing from you.

_Numbers: how many of the pair's example questions went to the other entry in the check described at the end (Hebrew and Russian: before → after the new example questions)._

## 1. Merge two entries into one (needs your OK)

### Merge 1: `glass-8mm-vs-10mm` + `shower-8-vs-10mm`

- `glass-8mm-vs-10mm` (What is the difference between 8 mm and 10 mm glass?)
- `shower-8-vs-10mm` (Should I choose 8 mm or 10 mm glass for my shower?)
- **Suggestion:** Keep `glass-8mm-vs-10mm` and retire `shower-8-vs-10mm`; move its example questions over.
- Why: The general answer already says everything the shower answer says (8 mm is the usual choice for frameless showers, 10 mm for very wide panels or heavy doors) and adds shelves. Today the two are not linked at all, so a visitor who gets the "wrong" one has no way to the other. If you prefer to keep both, approve the link in section 2 instead.
- Example questions lost to the other entry: he 12 → 10, en 16, ar 12, ru 15 → 3 (Hebrew and Russian: before → after the new example questions).

### Merge 2: `warranty-installations` + `warranty-what-covered`

- Why: Both answers give the same facts: 2 years, labour and materials, hinges, handles, sealing and installation quality, and a manufacturer's warranty on the glass. `warranty-all-products` (railings, mirrors, shop fronts…) stays separate.

## How the pairs were found

For each language, a fifth of every entry's example questions was hidden, and each hidden question was asked as if a visitor typed it; this was repeated five times so every example question was hidden once (about 3,400 per language). When a hidden question was answered by another entry, or the other entry came within a hair of it, that counted against the pair. Every pair with at least 15 such losses across the four languages, or 6 in Hebrew, is listed, plus the pairs we had already seen in the Hebrew tests.

## Technical follow-ups (for the developer, not content)

- **Product named → prefer the product entry.** A general entry (`glass-8mm-vs-10mm`, `glass-hardware-finishes`) is not held back when the visitor names a product that a close rival entry is about ("מקלחון 8 או 10"). The engine only penalises the reverse case.
- **Price questions → prefer the price entry.** "מחיר לקיר מראות בחדר הכושר" goes to the mirror-wall install entry, because price words are in so many entries that they weigh little.
- **"Cheap" is a price word.** "זול", "יקר" and "מחיר" share one synonym group, so "מעקה זכוכית זול" (a saving question) looks like "מחיר מעקה".
- **Hebrew prefixes.** "שמים" (we put) is read as "מים" (water), which pulls "איזו זכוכית שמים במקלחון" toward the water-sealing entry; "מניעת" (prevention) does not match "למנוע/מונעים".
- **After a merge.** Test questions in `tests/answer-engine/*.json` that expect a retired id must be relabelled to the kept id, by script (held-out and blind questions must not be printed), and `relatedIds` pointing to it redirected.
- **"Подешевле" is not a price word, and only the 60 best text matches are scored.** "подешевле стеклянные перила" matches every railing entry on "стеклянные перила", so the saving entry's documents can fall outside the 60 candidates that get a confidence score. Adding "подешевле" (and similar forms) to the price/saving synonyms, or scoring more candidates, would fix it.
- **Other languages.** Hebrew and Russian example questions are done; the same pairs still lose questions in English and Arabic (columns above), so the same work there is the next step.
